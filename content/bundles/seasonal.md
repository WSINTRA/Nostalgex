October is the one month of the year where the guide should be mostly one thing, and this is the bundle that does it.

Four channels, split by how much you want to be scared and who is in the room. SCREAM KIDS is the spooky season shelf for younger viewers: the friendly ghosts, the cartoon monsters, the ones with a happy ending. FRIGHT NIGHT is the video store horror aisle, the era of a masked guy and a camp, box art that was scarier than the movie. SCREAM ADULTS is the end of the shelf you do not put on before the kids are asleep. NOSTALGEX HORROR is the mixed channel: whatever horror is in your library, running all day, no sorting by subgenre.

It works best on exactly the night you want it to. Put SCREAM on, leave the Apple TV running, and the guide behaves like a channel that programmed a Halloween marathon without asking you for a list. People come and go, the schedule keeps running, and anyone walking in lands in the middle of something instead of staring at a menu while everyone waits.

Same rule as everywhere else in the app: these are your movies. Nostalgex reads the Plex or Jellyfin library you already own, groups the horror into these four channels, builds a schedule and starts playing mid film. If your horror shelf is thin, you will see fewer channels. If you have been collecting slashers for fifteen years, FRIGHT NIGHT will run for a week without repeating itself.

A nice thing about doing it this way: you stop rewatching the same three. Everyone has the Halloween picks they default to. A channel does not default. It digs out the one you bought on a whim in 2011 and never put on, and because it was already playing when you tuned in, you watch it.

The bundle switches on and off in settings like any other. Free, open source under the MIT license, Apple TV on tvOS 18 or later, Plex or Jellyfin.
