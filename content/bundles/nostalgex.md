This is the bundle the app is named after, and if you only switch one on, make it this one.

Nineteen channels, all pointed at the same stretch of time: roughly 1985 to 2005, back when you watched whatever was on because that was the deal. SAT MORNING CARTOONS runs the cartoons you ate cereal in front of. AFTER SCHOOL TV picks up where the bus dropped you off. 90S SITCOMS is the laugh track block, and Y2K SITCOMS carries it into the early 2000s. VHS VAULT and DVD SHELF are the two shelves everybody had, one of them taped off TV with the last ten minutes missing.

Then there are the channels that are less about a year and more about a mood. REWATCHABLES MOVIES and REWATCHABLES TV are the ones you have seen eleven times and will put on again tonight. BUDDIES is the Friday night cop movie channel: two guys who can't stand each other and one car that is about to get wrecked. LAST ACTION HEROES is the solo version of that. GIRLS NIGHT, TEEN MOVIES, CLASSIC FAM JAMS, NOSTALGEX LOL, ACTION ADVENTURE, RETRO TOONS, Y2K COMEDY, Y2K TOONS: each one is a shelf you can flip to without thinking about it.

Worth saying plainly, because people ask: Nostalgex does not bring any of these movies with it. Every channel is built from your own Plex or Jellyfin library. The app reads what you already own, sorts it into channels, builds a schedule and starts playing as if it had been running all day. A library heavy on 90s comedies gets a thick 90S SITCOMS channel. If you have never owned a cartoon, RETRO TOONS never appears. An empty channel is worse than a missing one, so a channel only turns on once there is enough on your server to fill it.

That is also why this is the bundle people leave on. It casts the widest net of the fourteen, so it keeps a guide full even when a library is small. And the schedule runs in real time. Tune in at 8:40 and you land twenty minutes into something, the way it used to work. No menus, no picking, nobody scrolling for twenty minutes before anyone watches anything.

Free, open source under the MIT license, and it runs on any Apple TV on tvOS 18 or later.
